Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the Hekaya Naqsa posting tool does with information.

The tool is used by the channel owner. It does not ask viewers for their name, email, or any other personal information.

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the tool receives a basic account id and an access token. These are stored only on the owner computer so the tool can post the approved short. They are not sold and not shared with anyone else.

The video file is uploaded to the owner TikTok account and to the owner YouTube channel, https://www.youtube.com/@HekayaNaqsa, after the owner approves it.

The owner can disconnect access in TikTok settings. Disconnecting stops future posts.
